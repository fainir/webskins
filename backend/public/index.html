A look you choose

Describe a style

Use your own Anthropic API key to ask for a different colour scheme, spacing or visual style. AI requests are billed by Anthropic.

Preview and refine

Review the generated CSS and refine the result with a follow-up request. Applying or removing a saved skin does not require AI.

Keep control

Choose which website origins may send page captures to AI, manage saved skins and remove the applied style when you want.

Understand what is shared

AI generation may send rendered page content, your prompt and screenshots to Anthropic after the extension asks for permission. Optional marketplace sharing is public. Read the version-specific details before using WebSkins on private pages.

Read the privacy policy

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics, Mixpanel and Betterloop stay off until you allow them in Analytics choices. If allowed, they receive basic page and fixed button events with random device identifiers or cookies. Advertising features, session replay and text capture are disabled. This applies only to this public website; it adds no tracking to your installed extension or the pages you browse. You can turn it off at any time; browser privacy signals keep it off.
